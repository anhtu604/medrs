## Operational use

Define the treatment/exposure conditions, target population, outcome variable and time point, handling of intercurrent events when relevant, and population-level summary before selecting an estimator. Sensitivity analysis examines the robustness of the same primary question to assumptions; an analysis answering a different question is supplementary and must be labeled accordingly.

A sample-size record must identify the objective, design, primary outcome, calculation method, test or interval target, sidedness, alpha, power or precision, effect/variance/prevalence assumptions and their provenance, allocation, design effect, attrition/missingness adjustment, software or formula version, and rounding. When assumptions are uncertain, present a labeled scenario grid and request an author decision.
