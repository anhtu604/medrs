# Viết Bàn luận

Inherit the [basic-statistics interpretation ceilings](../phan-tich-so-lieu/references/basic-statistics-contract.md): post-hoc cutoffs/models, unaddressed multiplicity, failed PH or unverified linkage stay qualified locally. Strength-led writing cannot convert exploratory associations into confirmatory or causal claims.

Follow the shared [working principles](../medrs/references/working-principles.md).
Select standalone/assembled/revision scope using [the shared gate lifecycle](../kiem-van-phong/references/gate-lifecycle.md).
When editing or writing into a Word file, follow the [Zotero field contract](../quan-ly-trich-dan/references/zotero-field-contract.md).

Read [references/discussion-workflow.md](references/discussion-workflow.md), then read the shared [writing preflight](../viet-phuong-phap/references/writing-preflight.md) once before delivery.

Use verified Results, the study design and its inferential ceiling, the source ledger, and the document-type profile. A target journal and comparator papers help but are optional.

Open each argument paragraph with its interpretation or meaning, then the evidence and comparison, following [the claims and openings rules](../kiem-van-phong/references/claims-and-openings.md). Use finding → meaning → contribution → comparison as a menu of useful argument moves, not mandatory sentences in every paragraph. Include a contribution only when supported and useful. Compare the nearest relevant original studies; explain differences only to the extent sources support. Check the direction of each explanation and preserve valuable comparisons when shortening. Keep general limitations in one profile-placed section, retaining local qualification needed to avoid misleading claims. Do not invent mitigation. Missing support stays as `[CẦN TRÍCH DẪN: …]` / `[SOURCE NEEDED: …]`. Return the draft, paragraph map, evidence-needs matrix, audit results and the closing checklist.
