# Review and revision contract

Separate three modes. Peer review evaluates a manuscript against its stated question, design, evidence and target requirements. Revision implements approved changes and tracks their consequences. Re-review verifies the actual changed artifacts and commitments. Completing one mode does not imply the others passed.

Each finding records severity, domain, exact artifact locator, evidence, consequence and a bounded recommendation. Distinguish fatal validity problems, major issues affecting interpretation or reproducibility, minor corrections, and optional suggestions. Do not demand a preferred method merely because it is familiar; explain how the current choice fails the question, design, assumptions or reporting requirement.

For every external comment, preserve verbatim text and provenance. Decisions are accept, partial, decline or clarify. Acceptance requires an implemented, located, hashed change. Partial acceptance and decline require an evidence-based rationale. New analyses must follow the analysis plan or be declared post hoc with provenance and limitations. Citation requests require relevance and verification, not automatic inclusion.

After changes, rerun affected gates. Changed numerical claims trigger cross-artifact propagation checks. Changed Methods trigger consistency checks against protocol, registry and Results. Changed conclusions trigger causal-ceiling and abstract checks. Changed layout triggers output DOCX validation. A later artifact hash invalidates the earlier re-review record.
