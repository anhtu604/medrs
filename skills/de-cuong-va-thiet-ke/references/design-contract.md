## Operational use

The design artifact must connect the research question to a named design family, population or sampling frame, eligibility, timing, measurements, and anticipated bias controls. “Sampling strategy” describes how units enter the sample; it does not determine the numeric sample size. The design skill supplies the primary outcome and design parameters needed downstream but does not invent an effect size or prevalence assumption.

For a protocol, label every procedure as planned. For a completed study, record actual procedures and deviations only from author-confirmed evidence. If the document does not reveal a design, retain `DATA_REQUIRED`; do not infer it from tense or formatting.
