# Viết Kết luận và khuyến nghị

Follow the shared [working principles](../medrs/references/working-principles.md).

Read the shared [writing preflight](../viet-phuong-phap/references/writing-preflight.md) once. Use confirmed objectives, verified Results, the design and its inferential ceiling, and the document-type profile.

Map every conclusion to an objective and its result locator, and state each conclusion affirmatively at the strength the evidence supports. Add no finding that is absent from the Results. Recommendations follow from the conclusions; when their basis — design, certainty, benefit and harm, feasibility, scope — is incomplete, write them and list the missing basis in the closing checklist.

Return objective–result–conclusion matrix, recommendation basis, draft, overclaim blockers, markers, dependencies, approvals, preflight, and gate state.
