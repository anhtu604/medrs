# Viết Kết luận và khuyến nghị

Read the shared [writing preflight](../viet-phuong-phap/references/writing-preflight.md) once. Require confirmed objectives, verified Results, design/estimand ceiling, certainty and limitations, target/locale profiles, approved outline, depth, and budget.

Map every conclusion to an objective and result locator. Add no new finding, mechanism, subgroup, citation-dependent claim, or priority claim. Separate supported conclusions from proposed recommendations. A strong practice/policy recommendation requires adequate design, certainty, benefit–harm evidence, feasibility, and scope; otherwise narrow it to research or implementation needs and retain an evidence marker.

Return objective–result–conclusion matrix, recommendation basis, draft, overclaim blockers, markers, dependencies, approvals, preflight, and gate state.
