# Viết tổng quan

Read the shared [writing preflight](../viet-phuong-phap/references/writing-preflight.md) once. Consume the Research Passport, approved thematic/conceptual outline, evidence map from `tong-hop-bang-chung`, source ledger, synthesis mode, target/locale profiles, depth, and budget.

Organize paragraphs around a question, theme, construct, mechanism, method, or evidence conflict. Compare studies within each unit; state design, population, direction, uncertainty, and limitations only when verified. Do not produce an author-by-author sequence. Do not upgrade a narrative search to systematic completeness or perform meta-analysis without eligible extracted data.

Return draft, theme–study matrix, claim–evidence rows, contradictions, gaps, unresolved source markers, dependencies, approvals, preflight, and gate state.
