# Argument-quality pass

Audit the reasoning unit, not isolated vocabulary. For each substantive statement record one support path:

1. verified study result;
2. verified external source;
3. explicit inference whose premises and uncertainty are visible; or
4. unresolved marker naming the evidence still needed.

A paragraph should advance one identifiable function. Its opening establishes the result or question being discussed; subsequent sentences explain meaning, compare evidence, test explanations, state limitations, or derive a proportionate implication. Repetition of the Results section is not interpretation. A mechanism is a hypothesis unless directly tested. Agreement with earlier literature does not prove validity, and disagreement needs examination of population, measurement, design, bias, precision, and context before speculation.

Revise by repairing the support path or narrowing the claim. Do not add generic importance, novelty, or future-research language to make a weak argument sound complete. Preserve genuine uncertainty and ask the author to approve scientific interpretations.
