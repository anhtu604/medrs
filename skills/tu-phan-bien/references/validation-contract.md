# Manuscript validation contract

Validation states are `PASS`, `REVISE`, `BLOCKED_SOURCE`, `BLOCKED_CONFLICT`, and `AUTHOR_APPROVAL_REQUIRED`. A pass names the actual artifact hash and all profiles/checklists used. Unsupported adapters and unavailable checks remain visible; they are not skipped as passes.

Compare each manuscript number and substantive claim with its Results artifact, source, or approved reasoning record. Compare design, population, dates, eligibility, sample flow, outcomes, models, denominators, estimates, uncertainty, missingness, harms, conclusions, registration, funding, and disclosures across title/abstract/text/tables/figures/supplements. Report both conflicting values and locators.

Load only the reporting guideline matching the confirmed design and article type. A named guideline is not enough: its coverage manifest must pass item/subitem identifiers, official source, version, license, last verification, freshness, and explicit caller checks. A missing item blocks guideline completion.
