# Current intervention and diagnostic appraisal tools

Load this reference for a nonrandomized intervention-effect estimate or a diagnostic accuracy estimate. Select the actual estimand before opening a checklist. An exposure-effect follow-up study belongs to the ROBINS-E workflow, not automatically to ROBINS-I. A prevalence estimate is not diagnostic accuracy merely because cases were identified by a test.

## ROBINS-I V2 draft: intervention follow-up studies

Primary: https://www.riskofbias.info/welcome/robins-i-v2. The currently posted revision is dated 20 November 2025 and explicitly remains draft, subject to change. Reverify within 180 days or on a reported update. Do not present V2 as finalized guidance, merge its numbering with 2016, or apply it silently when a review protocol pins a different version. A version change requires a recorded protocol decision and reappraisal of affected evidence.

Before domain responses complete the official preliminary result specification, target-trial/effect definition, triage and confounder evaluation. These preliminary sections and official decision algorithms are required use-time inputs; the manifest's declared scope is domain-signalling navigation, not a claim to redistribute the entire tool. An unclear comparison or missing target trial cannot be repaired by picking a risk-of-bias color.

Domain 1 has separate baseline/intention-to-treat and baseline-plus-time-varying/per-protocol variants. The navigation prefixes `1A` and `1B` disambiguate repeated official `1.x` identifiers; they are not new questions. Select the applicable variant using the original guidance. Domains 2–6 cover classification, participant selection, missing data, outcome measurement and reported-result selection. Follow the official conditional paths and response options, which can distinguish stronger and weaker judgments; do not reuse the simpler RoB 2 response set by default.

Assess classification and selection against actual follow-up onset to detect immortal-time/prevalent-user issues. Evaluate missing intervention, confounder and outcome information separately, distinguishing observed completeness from imputed values. Review analysis-plan availability and selective outcome/analysis/subgroup reporting. Every response needs a precise methods, protocol, data or supplement locator and rationale. Do not equate an unadjusted estimate with absence of confounding merely because the p-value is small.

The downloaded draft's question 4.4 repeats cross-reference `4.4` in its own condition. Preserve that source anomaly in the review record and seek official clarification; do not silently repair the algorithm from memory. Domain/overall judgments requiring that unresolved branch stay qualified. Official algorithms propose judgments; the reviewer must document justified departures rather than copy a reassuring output.

## QUADAS-3 v1.2: diagnostic accuracy estimates

Primary: https://www.bristol.ac.uk/population-health-sciences/projects/quadas/quadas-3/. Use the v1.2 original and Explanation & Elaboration before judging an estimate. QUADAS-2 remains an explicit protocol-pinned legacy choice, not a interchangeable label for a QUADAS-3 assessment.

Complete six phases: identify synthesis questions, define the ideal accuracy trial, record participant/test flow, identify the numerical estimates included in synthesis, assess each selected estimate, and record overall judgments. Review-level phases cannot be omitted because the study has a convenient 2x2 table. One study can contribute different index tests, thresholds, reference standards, subgroups and analysis units; link each judgment to the exact estimate instead of recycling a study-wide traffic light.

Phase 5 contains twenty signalling entries across participants, index test, target condition and analysis. Assess applicability for the first three domains separately from bias; analysis has no invented fourth applicability domain. Retain the source's response categories and reviewer rationale. A negative signalling response raises a concern but is not an automatic high-risk judgment; apply source guidance and question-specific relevance. Insufficient information is not low risk.

Validate the reconstructed numerator/denominator, missing references, uninterpretable tests, timing and participant/specimen analysis unit before handing sensitivity/specificity to synthesis. Overall judgments must remain estimate-specific. Reuse only unchanged domain evidence where the original permits it, and identify which assessment applies to which estimate. Missing source information stays visible and cannot be converted into precision or certainty by pooling.

## Record and handoff

Keep initial appraisals, disagreements, author decisions and corrected assessments in dated history. Return each applicable item response, evidence locator, official-tool provenance, chosen variant, domain rationale, overall judgment, unresolved items and sensitivity implications. Do not generate GRADE automatically from these labels. Reporting completeness, individual-estimate bias and body-of-evidence certainty are separate judgments with separate inputs.
